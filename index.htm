FAQ

Questions, answered.

Are the instruments scientifically validated?

Yes. Every scale in Psicotest26 is peer-reviewed, with documented norms and regular updates aligned to the latest research.

How is participant data protected?

All data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Access is role-based, and every action is recorded in a full audit trail.

Can I export reports to PDF?

Absolutely. Any report can be exported as a polished PDF or shared securely with collaborators in a single click.

Is there a free trial?

Yes. You can run your first assessments free, with no credit card required. Upgrade only when you are ready.
